Obsolescence Risk Ratings — education & training. Every French RNCP tech program, rated for exposure to becoming obsolete (a leading indicator: we map the risk zone, not predict the outcome). Every AI-exposure judgment reads the action's official ESCO definition, not its name — a discipline that moved 227 programs to a lower band and 73 higher vs the name-only build. We lead with AI exposure — the share of the actions a program teaches that current AI tools can automate (absolute; assessed only where we have tool evidence). Misalignment — fit to the demand of its own target occupations (median 3, averaged) — is relative and directional, so it never alone produces a worst grade. 499 rated · 167 unrated (insufficient evidence — abstained, never assumed safe).
