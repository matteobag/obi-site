How to read this page. One skill-demand index, per market — no primary market. Pick a country below: each panel is what that labour market asks for, skill by skill (% of its tech ads), same extraction engine and same ESCO perimeter everywhere. Where we have history the trend sparkline is shown (France 12,536 ads · 13 wk, Italy 1,597 ads · 9 wk); the newer panels (US 1,676 · UK 1,676) are a level snapshot with the weekly series just starting to accumulate. Sources differ by market (France = job-board WTTJ · Italy = Indeed+LinkedIn · US/UK = employer-direct ATS) so cross-market reads are directional; markets are kept separate, never blended.

Provenance & limits

Source: ATS employer-direct (Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby/SmartRecruiters), single pull 2026-W26 · level-only snapshot; per-market random samples; weekly series starts as the ATS cron accumulates.
Employer-direct ATS postings skew towards startup/scale-up tech employers (declared: this is a panel, not a census of the whole labour market).
Extraction: English-native (no translation), same linker, same threshold, same tech-ESCO perimeter as the French index — penetration levels are comparable in construction; residual source-mix differences are why cross-market comparisons are directional.
An education program that recruits internationally should be measured against the market its graduates actually enter — these panels are that reference.
