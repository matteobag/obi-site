2026 proof of concept — these numbers are frozen. This page documents the method as it ran on a single market (France, tech occupations). It is not updated. The collection has since grown to 15 countries and 11 million job postings, and no market is published as a live index yet — each goes live only when its coverage clears our gate. Methodology & data — what every number is built on. The scope, the counts and the limits are set out below. the recipe-level detail (models, thresholds, matching internals) is documented internally and not published: sources are disclosed under NDA and on audit.

Data

Demand — 12,536 French tech job ads, 2026-W14–2026-W27, weekly panel, single job-board source, full ad text. Levels/shares solid; trends directional (collection ramp) and labelled.
Education — 666 French RNCP tech certifications (official national registry, full text incl. activities & assessed capacities).
AI capability — 5,924 AI tools (structured capability descriptions, dated snapshot 2026-06-28), each judged against the official ESCO definition of every candidate work-action.
Salary — advertised band midpoints, ~16% of ads disclose pay (selective sample, directional).

Ratings

Two independent axes, shown as bands, never fused into one score: misalignment (fit of the program's skill profile to the observed demand of its declared target occupations — median 3 per program, averaged; relative percentile; band cut-offs = terciles of the live distribution, 0.5/0.642 at this build) × AI exposure (share of the program's taught actions that current AI tools can execute autonomously; absolute bands 40%/60%; evidence floor: ≥2 independent providers per action, ≥3 evidenced actions and ≥30% syllabus coverage per program).
499 rated · 167 unrated — where evidence is insufficient we abstain; absence of evidence is never scored as safe.
Named tools are shown as evidence (stack chips, syllabus column) but are not part of the rating bands: registry-style program descriptions systematically under-report tool names (measured), so rating on them would grade writing style, not curriculum.
Per-program ratings are directional: no external gold standard yet; syllabus writing style still affects the misalignment axis (declared; mitigated by the profile metric and multi-occupation anchoring). External convergence: occupation-level replace intensities fall inside published benchmark bands (data occupations 33–44%).

Known limits (declared, not hidden)

One market (FR) · 13 weeks · tech scope only · trend confounded by collection ramp (levels are solid) · AI exposure = capability of current tools, not observed adoption · mismatch has no external gold yet (mini-gold and insertion-rate validation planned) · salary coverage ~16% and selective · single AI-capability snapshot (trajectory needs the second dated pull).
