Skills mismatch — measured.

What a rating contains: where a programme sits against what the market is actually buying, how much of what it teaches AI can already do, and what it would take to close the gap. Below is a real rating and a real market — 499 education and training programmes — with every name removed.

Names are withheld by design. We publish the method and the distribution, never the names: a provider sees its own rating and its own gap list first. Programmes appear as Programme NNN and institutions as Institution NN; the pseudonyms are stable, so the portfolio view below is a true grouping — only the identities are gone.

What this is. A 2026 proof of concept on a partial sample: 499 French programmes, tech occupations, built from public programme descriptions. Ratings are directional — the signal is the band and the gap list, not the decimal. AI exposure is projected capability, not observed adoption: it maps the risk zone, it does not predict the outcome. Programmes with too little evidence are left unrated rather than assumed safe.
