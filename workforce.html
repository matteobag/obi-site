What we don’t claim. No source sees a labour market in full, so we publish coverage, shares and changes — never a count of the whole market. Nothing here is a published index yet: the pages you can open are a 2026 proof of concept on a partial sample, and each market goes live only once its coverage is high enough for us to stand behind it. AI exposure is projected capability, not observed adoption: it marks the risk zone, it does not predict the outcome. Programme and role reads are directional.
